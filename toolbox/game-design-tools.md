# Useful Game Design Links 
Here is a list of useful free and open source tools for game design and development. 
## Art
- [Blender](https://www.blender.org/) - 3D modeling and animation tool
- [Krita](https://krita.org/en/) - 2D vector graphics, digital painting, and animation
- [MagicaVoxel](https://ephtracy.github.io/) - A free lightweight GPU-based voxel art editor and interactive path tracing renderer.
- [Piskel](https://www.piskelapp.com/) - Online pixel art and animation tool 

## Design 
- [micro Game Design Document (mGDD)](https://github.com/Ada-Rhodes/micro-Game-Design-Document) - A free book to help you develop and refine an idea for a game.

## Music Production 
- [LMMS](https://lmms.io/) - A free and open-source digital audio workstation
- [Online Sequencer](https://github.com/Ada-Rhodes/micro-Game-Design-Document) - A simple browser-based sequencer for composing music
- [Signal](https://signalmidi.app/) - An open-sourced MIDI editor with a free online version. 
- [Strudel](https://strudel.cc/) - A unique code-based sequencer

## Sound Design 
- [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/) - A free tool for audio recording and editing
- [Cardboard Synth](https://www.gsn-lib.org/apps/cardboardsynth/index.html) - A tool for generating waveforms for sound effects and music production
- [JSFXR](https://sfxr.me/) - A browser-based tool for creating sound effects

## Story
- Story Bible Starter Booklet - A free tool for getting started on a game's story

# Team and Project Management
## Brainstorming
- [Miro](https://miro.com/online-whiteboard/) - A collaborative online whiteboard

## Project Management
- [Notion](https://www.notion.com/) - An online bullet journal that lets you make customized tools for tracking and allocating tasks
- [Trello](https://trello.com/) - An online Kanban (fancy to-do list)

# Assets Assets Assets Assets (Free to use assets)
- [Dev Assets](https://devassets.com/) - High quality assets on a "pay what you want" model
- [Game-Icons.net](https://game-icons.net/) - Free SVG icons for video and board games.
- [Itch.io](https://itch.io/game-assets/free) - Itch has a bunch of high-quality free and affordable assets of all kinds. 
- [Open Game Art](https://opengameart.org/) - Free 3D and 2D art, sounds, and music. 
- [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/) - Royalty-free images, vectors, music, sound effects, 3D models, and more!
