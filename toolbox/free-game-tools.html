Useful Game Design Links

Here is a list of useful free and open source tools for game design and development. For questions (and answers if you are lucky) reach out to Dr. Ada-Rhodes Wish at UNO.

Art

  • Blender - 3D modeling and animation tool
  • Krita - 2D vector graphics, digital painting, and animation
  • LibreSprite - Tidy and portable application for pixel art and animation
  • MagicaVoxel - A free lightweight GPU-based voxel art editor and interactive path tracing renderer.
  • Piskel - Online pixel art and animation tool
  • SculptGL - Free online 3D sculpting tool made by the dev behind Nomad Sculpt.

Design

  • micro Game Design Document (mGDD) - A free book to help you develop and refine an idea for a game.

Music Production

  • LMMS - A free and open-source digital audio workstation
  • Online Sequencer - A simple browser-based sequencer for composing music
  • Signal - An open-sourced MIDI editor with a free online version.
  • Strudel - A unique code-based sequencer

Sound Design

  • Audacity - A free tool for audio recording and editing
  • Cardboard Synth - A tool for generating waveforms for sound effects and music production
  • JSFXR - A browser-based tool for creating sound effects

Story

  • InkleWrite - Quick and easy tool for making interactive narratives.
  • Story Bible Starter Booklet - A free tool for getting started on a game's story.
  • Twine - An easy and quick tool for making interactive narrative.

Team and Project Management

Brainstorming

  • Miro - A collaborative online whiteboard

Project Management

  • Notion - An online bullet journal that lets you make customized tools for tracking and allocating tasks
  • Trello - An online Kanban (fancy to-do list)

Assets Assets Assets Assets (Free to use assets)

  • Dev Assets - High quality assets on a "pay what you want" model
  • Game-Icons.net - Free SVG icons for video and board games.
  • Itch.io - Itch has a bunch of high-quality free and affordable assets of all kinds.
  • Open Game Art - Free 3D and 2D art, sounds, and music.
  • Pixabay - Royalty-free images, vectors, music, sound effects, 3D models, and more!
